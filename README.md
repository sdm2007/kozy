# Kozy — JEE Study Companion 📚

**Kozy** is a web-based study companion designed for JEE preparation. It helps students track study time, manage tasks, monitor syllabus progress, visualize study patterns, and keep track of upcoming exams — all in one place.

🔗 **Live Demo:** *[(https://sdm2007.github.io/kozy/)]*

---

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Dashboard

* Total study time
* Today's study time
* Study sessions completed today
* Most-studied subject
* Daily study goal
* Current study streak
* Best study streak

### ⏱️ Study Timer

* Track focused study sessions
* Associate study sessions with subjects
* Automatically record study duration
* Build a history of study activity

### ✅ To-Do List

* Create and manage study tasks
* Keep track of pending and completed work

### 📈 Study Reports

* View study time over different periods
* Visualize study minutes over time
* See how study time is distributed across subjects
* Uses charts to make study patterns easier to understand

### 📚 Syllabus Tracker

* Add subjects
* Add individual chapters
* Track three stages for each chapter:

  * Theory
  * Practice
  * Revision
* Calculate subject-wise and overall syllabus completion

### 📝 Exam Tracker

* Add upcoming exams
* Associate exams with subjects
* Set exam dates
* Automatically calculate days remaining
* Separate upcoming and past exams

### 🌙 Personalization

* Light and dark themes
* Collapsible sidebar
* User-specific study data

### 🔐 Authentication & Data

* User authentication
* Study data can be synchronized across browsers/devices using Supabase

## 💡 Why I Built Kozy

I originally started working on this project during my JEE preparation.

While preparing, I wanted a single place where I could track not just *how long* I studied, but also **what I studied, how much of my syllabus was actually completed, what my study patterns looked like, and how close my exams were.**

The project was left incomplete during my preparation, but I later recovered it, rebuilt parts of it, and brought it to a working, publicly accessible version.

This project is also a record of my early journey into software development and my interest in building tools around problems I personally experienced.
